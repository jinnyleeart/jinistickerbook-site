About

A sticker app, built for artists.

Hi, I'm James! I'm an artist and I made jini stickerbook: a sticker app where people can download hand-drawn sticker packs to use in their chats, and a place where artists can earn from their work.

Other sticker apps I've come across put everything behind a subscription and pay the artists nothing. I wanted something that works for both sides: artists get paid for what they make, and people get stickers they actually connect with.

We're starting in iMessage, with plans to add more apps as we grow.

FAQ

The questions we'd ask too.

How much can I expect to make?

It depends on your audience and how your work connects. We're new and still building one, so we'd rather not throw out numbers we can't back up. What we can promise is that the split favors you, and that you can leave whenever you want.

What does 80% of net revenue mean?

Net revenue is what's left after Apple takes its cut of every sale (15%). You get 80% of that. On a $1.99 pack it works out to about $1.35 to you.

Why do you take 20%?

It helps cover the cost of running the app: building it, hosting the sticker files, and Apple's developer fees.

Can I list my stickers for free?

Yes, and we encourage it! A free pack is one of the best ways to connect with your supporters and get your work into people's everyday conversations. We make nothing on free packs, and we don't use your art for any other purpose.

How do I get paid?

Payouts run through PayPal monthly. We're setting up the payment and agreement paperwork now, artists will get both before anything of theirs goes on sale.

What files do you need?

Any size, any format. Send what you already have and we'll resize and compress it to fit Apple's sticker spec. We prefer square PNGs or APNGs with transparent backgrounds, but anything works.

Is there anything I can't publish?

One rule, really: the work has to be your own. That means no fan art of characters you don't own, no brand logos or trademarks, no recognizable real people who haven't agreed to it, nothing traced from another artist, and nothing AI-generated. Apple's rules sit on top of ours, so nothing hateful, explicit, or illegal either.

If you're unsure about an idea, just ask!

What if I want to leave?

Email or DM us and your packs come down. People who already bought one keep it, and you're still paid for sales made before you left.

Is my art safe with you?

Your files are used to build your sticker packs and nothing else — no sublicensing, no reselling, no model training.
