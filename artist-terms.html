Artist Program Terms

Version 1.0 · Last updated: August 2, 2026

These Artist Program Terms cover the day-to-day details of listing your sticker packs with jinistickerbook — what files to send, how prices work, and how and when you get paid. They are part of the Artist Agreement you signed, and they should be read alongside it.

These are the details most likely to change as we grow, which is why they live here rather than in the agreement itself. We will email you at least 30 days before any change takes effect, and no change ever applies to sales that already happened. Your revenue split and the license you granted us are set in the Artist Agreement and can only be changed with your written agreement — never through this page.

Sending your art

Send whatever you already have. We prepare the final files and cut them to Apple's sticker requirements, so you don't need to resize or export anything yourself.

  • Any format works. PNG, JPEG, PSD, PROCREATE, SVG, or a link to a folder.
  • Easiest for us: square PNGs with transparent backgrounds, 1000×1000 pixels or larger.
  • Animated stickers: send the numbered frames or the source file, and tell us the frame rate you want.
  • Pack size: most packs are between 8 and 30 stickers. There's no hard limit — talk to us if you have something bigger in mind.

We only ever change your files technically: resizing, cropping, compressing, and exporting to Apple's format. We don't redraw, recolor, or edit your art.

Approval

Before anything goes on sale, we send you the finished pack — every sticker, the pack artwork, and the listing text — for you to approve. Nothing is published until you say yes. If something doesn't look right after compression, tell us and we'll redo it.

Prices

You choose your pack's price from these tiers:

  • Free
  • $0.99
  • $1.99
  • $2.99

You can change your price at any time by telling us — the new price takes effect once Apple processes it, usually within a day or two. We will not change your price, or give your pack away for free, without asking you first.

Payouts

  • Method: PayPal, sent to the address you give us during onboarding.
  • Currency: US dollars. If your account is in another currency, PayPal's conversion and transfer fees are deducted by PayPal, not by us.
  • Schedule: monthly. Apple pays us roughly 30 to 45 days after a month closes, and we send payouts within 10 days of receiving Apple's payment. In practice, a sale made in January is paid out in early-to-mid March.
  • Minimum: $20. If your balance is under that, it rolls over to the next month. Nothing expires, and if you leave we pay out whatever you're owed regardless of the minimum.

To change your payout details, email us. Changes take effect from the next scheduled payout.

Your monthly statement

Every payout comes with a statement showing, for each of your packs: units sold, gross sales, Apple's commission, net revenue, your 80% share, and any balance carried over from last month. If something looks wrong, tell us within 60 days and we'll look into it.

Tax forms

Before your first payout we need a completed W-9 if you're in the United States, or a W-8BEN if you're outside it. Both are short, and we'll send you the form and instructions when you're ready to be paid. You're responsible for your own taxes on what you earn.

Taking a pack down

Email or DM us and we'll stop selling the pack, normally within 3 business days. People who already downloaded it keep it, and you're paid for everything that sold beforehand on the normal schedule. If your art appears in the App Store listing or in marketing we've already published, removing it can take up to 30 days where an app update or App Store review is needed.

Changes to this page

When we update these terms, we'll email everyone at least 30 days ahead of time, post the new version here with a new version number and date, and keep the previous version available on request. If you don't want to continue under the new terms, you can leave before they take effect.
