Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

jinistickerbook (“we,” “us”) provides the jinistickerbook iMessage sticker app (the “App”). This policy explains what information the App does and does not collect. In plain terms: the App is built to work without collecting personal information about you.

Information we do not collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Sticker packs you download, along with your favorites, likes, and how you’ve arranged your library, are stored locally on your device (and shared with the Messages extension so your stickers are available there). This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Information handled by our sticker service

The App loads sticker packs, stickers, and artist information from our hosting provider (Supabase). These are ordinary content requests. When you download a pack, the App records an anonymous count that a download occurred, so we can understand which packs are popular. This count is not linked to you or your device and does not identify you.

Purchases

Some sticker packs are free and some are paid. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID. We never see or receive your payment card details. Your purchase and refund information is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The App reads your purchase status from Apple only to unlock the packs you’ve bought and to restore purchases.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect personal information, no such information about children is gathered.

Data retention and security

Because we do not collect personal information about you, there is no personal profile for us to retain or delete. Content requests to our sticker service are transmitted over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights regarding personal data. Since we do not collect personal data through the App, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@jinistickerbook.com.
